## 👋 About me

**Computer Science student focused on Data Analytics and Business Intelligence.**

I build end-to-end analytics projects: from raw data and cleaning, through SQL querying, to dashboards that answer actual business questions. Below is a three-part retail portfolio where **the same business is analysed with Python, SQL and Power BI** — one dataset of 1,000 retail transactions, three different toolchains, three different angles on the same questions.

<details>
<summary><b>📈 Retail Power BI Dashboard</b> — <i>data modeling, DAX and dashboard storytelling</i></summary>

<br />

The third stage of the workflow: the same dataset, rebuilt as a proper BI solution.

**Four analytical pages**

- **Executive Overview** — KPI cards, monthly sales trend, sales by category, profit by payment method
- **Sales Analysis** — product and category performance, profitability breakdown
- **Customer Analysis** — repeat customer rate, loyalty segmentation, top customers by revenue
- **Inventory Analysis** — low-stock and out-of-stock detection, high-profit items at risk

**Built with** — star schema data model, DAX measures, Power Query transformations, cross-page interactive filtering.

**The business questions it answers** — Is the business growing? Which products and customers drive revenue? How loyal is the customer base? What needs replenishing?

➡️ **[Open the repository](https://github.com/fdicianni7813/retail-powerbi-dashboard)**

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🗄️ Retail SQL Analytics</b> — <i>querying a relational database like a Junior Data Analyst</i></summary>

<br />

A SQLite database queried to answer business questions, not just to practise syntax.

**What it analyses**

- Sales performance and revenue by category
- Product-level profitability ranking
- Inventory monitoring and stock-depletion risk
- Whether high-profit products are adequately stocked
- Most valuable customers, segmented by profitability

**SQL techniques** — aggregations and `GROUP BY`, `CASE WHEN` segmentation, CTEs, views for reusable reporting logic, multi-condition business filters.

**Stack** — SQLite · DB Browser for SQLite · VS Code

➡️ **[Open the repository](https://github.com/fdicianni7813/retail-sql-analytics)**

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🐍 Retail BI Dashboard</b> — <i>the full Python workflow, with a live dashboard you can try</i></summary>

<br />

Where the dataset is born: generation, cleaning, exploratory analysis, then an interactive Streamlit app.

**Dashboard features**

- **Sales Dashboard** — KPI cards and interactive Plotly charts
- **Inventory Analysis** — low-stock alerts and critical inventory checks
- **Upload Analysis** — drop in any CSV and get instant insights
- **Sidebar filters** by category and payment method
- **Dynamic business messages** driven by the performance indicators

**Stack** — Python · Pandas · NumPy · Plotly · Matplotlib · Streamlit · Jupyter Notebook
